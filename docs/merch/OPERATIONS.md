# Merch and staff access

- Public shop: `/`. Price MVR 550, free same-day delivery, cash on delivery/pickup or transfer to Bank of Maldives, ULDUZ PRIVATE LIMITED, MVR account configured privately in the management screen. USD transfers are not offered for MVR-priced orders.
- Staff: `/staff/` redirects to `/staff/login.html` until signed in.
- First owner: use the existing Netlify `ADMIN_PIN` once on the setup form, select the existing team name, and choose an individual username and 12+ character password. Setup closes atomically after the first active super admin exists. The old shared PIN login API is retired. `SESSION_SECRET` remains required.
- Existing data: original `lemonade-bros` Blobs store and sales/admin keys retained. New users, catalogue, orders and stock are held in `commerce-v1` in the same store. Do not delete or replace the store on deploy.
- Roles: employee = lemonade till, history and race; admin = same plus merch orders, product, stock and payment verification; superadmin = same plus original financial panel, expenses, team and account administration.
- Team → names and Accounts → credentials are distinct. Add an active team name before creating their account. Removed team names remain in historical sales. Disable their account to revoke access immediately. Changing role, password or active state revokes prior sessions.
- Staff credentials: accounts are explicitly created by a super admin; there are no default employee passwords. Initial/reset temporary passwords require a personal password change before entering staff tools. Share them privately. Passwords use salted PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations. Sessions are signed, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict, 8 hours, with server-side version checks. Auth writes require same-origin POST and attempts are limited.
- Bank account numbers are not stored in the public source repository. Enter the MVR account number once in the private management screen to enable transfer checkout. Stock starts at zero. In Merch & accounts → T-shirt & stock, enter actual inventory for each kids/adult size and save. Positive stock, published product and enabled ordering are required before customers can buy. Product images and actual size measurements/materials are intentionally pending. No fake stock or measurement data is seeded.
- Stock and order creation share a conditional write: retries reuse the same ID and cannot double-charge quantities. Product saves carry a catalogue revision and reject stale stock. Order cancellation returns inventory exactly once. Paid orders must be marked refunded before cancellation. Actual money transfers/refunds happen outside this application.
- COD orders start unpaid. Transfer orders also start unpaid; no automated bank verification or card gateway is configured. An admin must verify the payment and mark it paid. Only paid, non-cancelled merch orders count toward confirmed revenue. Unpaid totals remain separate.
- Customer details: names, phone numbers and delivery addresses are visible only to admins and super admins via the authenticated management API. Public endpoints never return orders, user records or customer details. No email/SMS notification service is connected; staff check Orders for new orders.
- Reconciliation: Super Admin → Sales log → Add cash record accepts a seller, amount and reason. It creates a private, idempotent Cash revenue record with zero known cups and a reconciliation note. It never invents flavour counts or inflates the cup race. The original sales remain unchanged. No live reconciliation amounts are seeded in the public source.
- Seller contributions: top sellers rank by confirmed revenue. Estimated allocation of net result is seller revenue / total revenue × (total revenue − all logged expenses). A negative result is labelled an estimated loss share. This is an allocation of shared costs, not measured per-seller product margin. Paid merch is attributed to the staff member who verifies payment.

## Local validation

`npm test` exercises authentication, first-owner setup, roles, password rotation, account disablement, protected finance/customer data, seller attribution, data retention, one-time reconciliation, order retry identity, simultaneous stock reservations, stale product writes, payment revenue, refund and restock behavior.

`npm run dev` uses a disposable in-memory store at localhost:4500, never live sales. Set LOCAL_PREVIEW_PIN in your local shell if you want to exercise owner setup; no default local credential is bundled.

Browser automation could not launch in the execution sandbox (Chromium socket operation denied), and the separate browser cannot reach localhost. Verify the actual published shop and login visually before announcing final delivery. Do not claim real iPhone testing.
